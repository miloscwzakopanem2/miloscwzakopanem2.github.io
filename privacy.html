Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Midnight Stories ("the App") is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish the owner's own videos to TikTok and YouTube.

Data we access

When the owner authorizes the App, it receives OAuth access tokens and basic account information (such as the account's open ID, display name and avatar) solely to upload videos to that account.

How data is used

Tokens are stored locally on the owner's private machine and are used only to upload videos. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Tokens can be revoked at any time from the TikTok or Google account settings, after which the locally stored copies are deleted.

Children

The App is not directed to children.
